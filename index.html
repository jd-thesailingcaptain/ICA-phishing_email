Welcome to EML Analyser

A safe practice inbox for spotting phishing. You examine realistic emails the way a security analyst would, then decide whether each one is phishing or legitimate.

  1. Open an email from the inbox on the left.
  2. Investigate it. Read the Message, check the Headers (From, Reply-To, Return-Path and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results), compare each link's text with its real destination in Links, and look at Attachments.
  3. Decide. Click PHISHING or LEGITIMATE. You get one answer per email, and the result is named True Positive, True Negative, False Positive or False Negative.
  4. Watch your score in the top right. Answer all 10 emails to see your final results. Reset (left side) starts over.
  5. Use your own emails. Add .eml files with the button or by dragging them onto the page, and remove any email with Remove.
Safe by design: everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Links are disabled, attachments are never opened, and every company and domain in the sample emails is fictional.
